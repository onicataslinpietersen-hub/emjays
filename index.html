Your new canteen right here.

EmJay's started with a simple idea: you shouldn't have to walk down the road for slap chips, order takeaways and promise yourself it's the last time, or settle for a sad desk lunch.

So we opened a proper canteen at The Queens in Wynberg. Half-smashed burgers, thick nippy wors, masala steak, gatsbys built to share and a different meal of the day, every day. All halaal, all made fresh.

Come sit with us, order to your office, or join one of our pool and dominos tournaments. Better food, better mood.
